Popular subject areas

Admission essentials

Requirements differ by course and institution; check the latest details before applying.

  • Admission from a recognised university
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • IELTS or other accepted English test
  • Proof of funds for the country you choose
  • Health insurance

Budget & living costs

Tuition and living expenses vary by location, institution and lifestyle. Plan for tuition, accommodation, food, transport, insurance and visa-related costs.

  • Tuition (varies hugely by country)
  • Living-cost proof
  • Visa and residence permit fees
  • Accommodation

Visas & working rights

Visa rules, work permissions and post-study pathways can change and depend on your circumstances. Always check the relevant government and institution guidance before making plans.

  • Each country has its own embassy process and checklist
  • Some countries are handled from embassies outside Bangladesh
  • Start early: appointment waiting times can be long
  • Your residence permit allows short trips within Schengen

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions, clear next steps.

Which European country is best for Bangladeshi students?

It depends on your budget, subject and English score. Italy and Finland are popular for low or scholarship-supported tuition, and Malta for English-speaking life. We compare options with you.

Can I study in Europe without IELTS?

Some universities accept other proof of English, but most embassies and universities prefer IELTS. Italy’s embassy in Dhaka, for example, asks for IELTS.

How much money do I need to study in Europe?

Each country sets its own figure. For example, Italy asks for €10,179.85 a year and Finland €800 a month.

Can I work while studying in Europe?

Most EU countries allow part-time work of around 20 hours a week; Finland allows an average of 30 hours.

Can I stay in Europe after graduation?

Most countries offer a job-search permit, for example 9 months in Malta, up to 12 months in Italy and up to 2 years in Finland.
