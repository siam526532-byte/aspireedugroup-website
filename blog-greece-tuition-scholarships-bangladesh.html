Greece tuition fees and scholarships for Bangladeshi students (2026 guide)

Real tuition ranges in euro and taka, the official Greek scholarships, who can apply and how to plan your budget.

Greece is one of the lower-cost study options in the European Union, but "low cost" does not mean free for Bangladeshi students. Greek-taught bachelor’s degrees are free, while the English-taught programmes most international students choose charge tuition. This guide shows the published fee ranges, converts them to taka, and explains the scholarships that actually exist, with the official links so you can check every figure yourself. Taka amounts use €1 ≈ ৳142 (Wise mid-market rate, 26 November 2026) and are rounded; your bank’s rate will differ.

Fee ranges come from the official Study in Greece portal, the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTh) and QS Top Universities. Always confirm the fee on your exact programme page.

Why English-taught programmes cost money

Public Greek universities do not charge EU/EEA students for Greek-taught undergraduate degrees, and Greek-taught bachelor’s programmes are offered free. The European Commission notes that fees may apply for English-taught bachelor’s and master’s programmes, and these are the programmes most Bangladeshi students apply to, because Greek-taught degrees require a B2-level Greek certificate. If you are willing to learn Greek, the tuition-free route exists, but it takes at least a year of language study first.

Bachelor’s vs master’s: where the money goes

A bachelor’s degree is usually the expensive route: English-taught programmes are priced per year, and four years add up. Master’s programmes are often priced for the whole degree, and QS reports that most charge a total of €1,500–€4,000, with some offered tuition-free. For many Bangladeshi graduates, a Greek master’s therefore costs less in tuition than one year of a bachelor’s. The visa also asks you to show that at least one-third of the tuition is already paid, so a lower total fee also means a smaller upfront payment.

Scholarship 1: Hellenic Ministry of Foreign Affairs (50 scholarships)

The Greek Ministry of Foreign Affairs awards fully funded scholarships for undergraduate and postgraduate study at public Greek universities to citizens of third countries. For 2026/2027 it announced 50 scholarships in June 2026. Key points from the official page:

  • Covers the standard length of the programme plus two years, subject to academic performance
  • Includes one year of free Greek language courses before undergraduate study, where needed
  • Greek-taught study requires a B2 Greek certificate; the language year is meant to get you there
  • There is an official list of eligible countries with a set number of scholarships per country
  • Applicants must not hold another scholarship at the same time
Check before you plan on it: eligibility depends on the country list attached to each year’s call. Download the current list from the Ministry’s page and confirm that Bangladesh is included before you apply; we do not assume it is.

Scholarship 2: IKY (State Scholarships Foundation)

IKY is Greece’s national scholarship body. Its programme for foreign nationals pays a monthly stipend of €600 (≈ ৳85,200) for master’s or postdoctoral study at a Greek university, for up to 36 months, and is open to nationals of any country. However, IKY has said that the most recent round for foreign nationals was not open, and previous deadlines fell on 16 June. Treat it as a possible bonus, not as your funding plan, and check iky.gr each spring.

Scholarship 3: University fee reductions

Some Greek universities offer their own reductions on English-taught programmes. For example, the AUTh environmental programme lists several pathways to reduced tuition on its fees page. These are set by each programme, so ask the admissions office directly what reductions exist, what grades they require and when you must apply, and get the answer in writing.

How to apply for Greek scholarships: practical steps

  • Pick an eligible programme at a public Greek university first; the MFA scholarships only cover public institutions
  • Download that year’s call, application form and eligible-country list from the Ministry’s page
  • Prepare certified, translated academic records and a strong statement of purpose
  • Apply through the Greek embassy channel named in the call, before its deadline
  • Keep your own funding plan ready: the visa still requires proof of funds unless the scholarship letter covers them

Frequently asked questions

Is study in Greece free for Bangladeshi students? Only Greek-taught programmes are tuition-free, and they require B2 Greek. English-taught programmes charge fees.

What is the cheapest way to study in Greece? A public-university master’s, where most programmes charge €1,500–€4,000 in total.

Are there fully funded scholarships? Yes, the Hellenic MFA scholarships are fully funded, but they are limited in number and depend on the eligible-country list for each year.

Can I get a scholarship for a private college? The MFA scholarships are for public Greek higher education institutions only.
