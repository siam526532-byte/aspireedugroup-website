Italy student visa from Bangladesh (2026): requirements and how to apply

Every requirement for the Italian study visa from Dhaka in 2026, in the order you need them.

Italy is one of the most popular European destinations for Bangladeshi students: public universities charge income-based fees, regional scholarships exist, and many master’s programmes are taught in English. The visa, however, is strict and the Embassy of Italy in Dhaka handles a very large number of applications. This guide sets out the 2026–27 requirements step by step, based on the Embassy’s published notice and official portals linked at the end.

How to apply for an Italy student visa from Bangladesh: 8 steps

  1. Check your eligibility (HSC or bachelor’s)
  2. Apply to Italian universities
  3. Complete Universitaly pre-enrolment
  4. Get a CIMEA certificate or Declaration of Value (DoV)
  5. Prepare €10,179.85 financial proof and sponsor documents
  6. Take IELTS: the Dhaka checklist asks for an overall score of at least 6
  7. Collect the other documents (insurance, accommodation)
  8. Submit at VFS Dhaka and wait for the Embassy’s decision

Step 1: Check that you are eligible

For a bachelor’s degree Italian universities require a school-leaving qualification after at least 12 years of schooling, which Bangladesh’s SSC plus HSC meets; some set a minimum result, for example the University of Turin lists HSC with Second Division or higher. For a master’s you need a bachelor’s degree in a related subject; Italian universities check the content of your previous degree, so a four-year honours degree in a matching field gives you the most options. See our guide to studying abroad after HSC.

Step 2: Apply to universities

Most Italian universities open applications for English-taught programmes in the first half of the year for a September/October start, and some close earlier for non-EU applicants. Each university evaluates you separately, so apply to two or three that fit your background. Keep translations of your transcripts and certificates ready, because Italian universities and the Embassy both need them.

Step 3: Complete Universitaly pre-enrolment

After the university accepts you, complete pre-enrolment on the official Universitaly portal. The university validates it, and this validated pre-enrolment is what allows you to apply for the visa. For 2026–27 the Embassy has said that students regularly registered on Universitaly are contacted by VFS Global by email to book their appointment, using the same email address given on Universitaly. Use an email you check daily.

Step 4: CIMEA certificate or Declaration of Value

The Embassy requires either a CIMEA certificate or a Declaration of Value (DoV) to prove that your study certificate is authentic. A DoV is issued by the Embassy; a CIMEA certificate is issued online by the Italian information centre. Start this early, because it can take weeks and your university may also ask for it.

Step 5: Financial proof and sponsors

The minimum for 2026–27 is €10,179.85 per year. For this cycle the Embassy accepts financial support from the applicant or from relatives such as parents, grandparents, siblings, uncles, aunts and cousins, including relatives living in Italy or Italian citizens. Whoever sponsors you, the funds must be documented and explainable: the Embassy looks at where the money came from, not only the closing balance. Sudden large deposits without a clear source are a common reason for refusal.

Step 6: Language certificate

For the visa the Embassy accepts only language certificates from globally recognised institutes or universities that clearly state your level in oral comprehension, written comprehension, speaking and writing. For English, the Dhaka embassy checklist goes further: it accepts IELTS only, with an overall score of at least 6, issued no more than two years before your visa application. An MOI (medium of instruction) letter does not meet this rule, even if a university accepted it for admission. Read more in our guide to studying without IELTS.

Step 7: Other documents

Prepare your passport and visa application form, the validated Universitaly pre-enrolment, academic documents with translation, CIMEA or DoV, financial documents, accommodation details and health insurance with at least €30,000 cover. Follow the latest checklist published on the Embassy website for your course type, because the Embassy updates it.

Step 8: Submit at VFS and wait for the decision

Submit your application at VFS Global in Dhaka on your appointment date. VFS only collects applications; the Embassy decides. The last day to submit study-visa applications for the 2026–27 academic year is 30 November 2026. Do not leave it to the final weeks: appointments are limited and missing documents cannot be added once the deadline passes.

After arrival: permesso di soggiorno

Within 8 days of arriving in Italy you must apply for your residence permit (permesso di soggiorno) for study. Keep copies of everything you submitted for the visa, your enrolment confirmation and your insurance.

Tuition, scholarships and part-time work

Public universities set fees according to family income through the ISEE parificato, so many students pay low fees. Regional DSU scholarships can cover tuition and part of living costs for students who meet income and merit criteria, and the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs also announces grants for foreign students through the Embassy. With a study residence permit you can work up to 20 hours a week and a maximum of 1,040 hours a year, and after graduating you can apply for a job-search permit of up to 12 months.

Why Italian study visas get refused

  • Funds that cannot be explained, or a sponsor outside the accepted relatives
  • Documents that contradict each other (dates, names, amounts)
  • A course with no logical link to your previous studies, or a long unexplained gap
  • A language certificate other than IELTS, or an IELTS overall score below 6
  • Missing CIMEA/DoV or an un-validated pre-enrolment
  • Any false or altered document
The Embassy does not publish an official approval rate for Bangladesh; see our honest guide to the Italy visa ratio.

Frequently asked questions

How much bank balance is needed for an Italy student visa? €10,179.85 per year of study for 2026–27, with a clear source of funds.

Is IELTS mandatory for Italy? For English-taught courses, the Dhaka embassy checklist accepts IELTS only, with an overall score of at least 6, issued within two years.

Can my uncle in Italy sponsor me? For 2026–27 the Embassy accepts relatives including uncles, aunts and cousins, including those living in Italy.

What is the deadline? 30 November 2026 for the 2026–27 academic year.

Can I work in Italy as a student? Yes, up to 20 hours a week and 1,040 hours a year.
