Greece student visa from Bangladesh: complete 2026 guide (requirements, costs, steps)

Every requirement for the Greek national D study visa from Dhaka, what it costs, and how to prepare a strong file.

Greece has become one of the most affordable ways for Bangladeshi students to study in the European Union. Public universities now offer English-taught degrees, tuition is lower than in most of Western Europe, and a Greek residence permit lets you travel across the Schengen area. The visa process, however, is different from Italy or Malta: your application is submitted in Dhaka but decided by the Greek Embassy in New Delhi. This guide explains every step, document and cost as of November 2026, based on the official visa centre and EU sources listed at the end.

Step 1: Choose an English-taught programme

According to the European Commission, Greek universities offer more than 200 English-taught degree programmes, including bachelor’s, master’s, joint and dual degrees. Most Greek-language bachelor’s degrees are free for EU students, but as a Bangladeshi student you will normally apply for an English-taught programme that charges tuition. Choose a course that follows logically from your previous studies; a clear academic link makes your visa file much stronger. The official Study in Greece portal lists international programmes and their application procedures.

Which Greek universities teach in English?

English-taught programmes are offered by public universities such as the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA), the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTh), the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), and the universities of Patras and Crete, as well as by recognised private colleges. Before you apply, make sure the institution is approved by the Greek Ministry of Education, because the visa centre asks for proof of this approval.

Step 2: Get your admission and pay part of the tuition

Once a university accepts you, ask for the admission documents in English. The Dhaka visa centre lists three university-related requirements: the admission documents, a direct email from the Greek university to the consular section confirming the visa type, and proof that the Greek Ministry of Education approves the institution’s operation. You must also show that you have paid at least one-third of your tuition fee. Ask the university early for the direct email to the embassy, because it is easy to forget and its absence delays files.

Step 3: Prepare the documents

The study visa checklist of the Visa Application Center for Greece in Dhaka includes:

  • Completed long-stay (national) visa application form
  • Two photos, 3.5 × 4 cm, white or off-white background, taken within the last 6 months
  • Passport valid for at least 1 year and 6 months from the submission date, in good condition
  • Proof of funds for living costs in Greece (at least €400 per month)
  • Original police clearance certificate with apostille, issued within 3 months of submission and attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka
  • Travel insurance valid for the first year
  • Proof of visa fee payment, made before submission
  • Admission documents, the university’s direct email to the consular section, and the Ministry of Education approval of the institution
  • Proof of your previous education
  • Course details, including whether it is taught in Greek or English
  • Proof of payment of at least one-third of the tuition
Start the police clearance early: it must be apostilled and attested, and it is only accepted if it is less than 3 months old when you submit.

Step 4: Show the right financial proof

The minimum is at least €400 for each month of study. For a two-year master’s that is €9,600 for living costs, on top of the remaining tuition. Officers look not only at the balance but at where the money came from, so keep the account history clear and be ready to explain large deposits. A sponsor’s income documents should match the size of the funds shown.

Step 5: Submit in Dhaka, interview and medical

You submit the application at the Visa Application Center for Greece in Dhaka, and the file is sent to the Embassy of Greece in New Delhi, which makes the decision. After submission an interview at the Greek Embassy is scheduled, and a medical examination follows the interview; the report goes directly to the embassy. The visa centre does not publish a fixed processing time for study visas, so apply as soon as your admission and documents are ready.

Always confirm current fees with the visa centre before paying, because they can change without notice.

Tuition fees and living costs in Greece

Tuition depends on the university and programme. Course-finder data puts English-taught programmes for non-EU students at roughly €1,500–€9,000 a year, while the official Study in Greece portal quotes €5,000–€13,000 a year for English-taught bachelor’s programmes. Master’s programmes are often cheaper in total. Living costs are commonly estimated at €450–€800 a month, lower than Italy, France or Germany, with Athens more expensive than cities such as Patras or Ioannina. Use these as planning ranges and check the exact fee on your programme’s page.

Can I study in Greece without IELTS?

Each university sets its own English requirement. Most English-taught programmes ask for IELTS, TOEFL or a similar test, and some accept other proof such as previous English-medium study. The visa centre checklist does not list IELTS itself, but it does ask whether your course is taught in Greek or English, and your admission must be valid. If your university accepts an MOI letter, keep it ready, and prepare for an interview where your English will be tested. When in doubt, a good IELTS score makes both admission and the visa easier.

Can I work while studying in Greece?

Yes. Under the EU Immigration Portal, a student in Greece can work part-time. Greece’s new migration law, Law 5275/2026, raises the weekly limit for students from 15 to 20 hours and allows graduates to stay for one year to look for work or start a business. Work must not interfere with your studies, and you become subject to Greek labour, tax and social security rules. Do not plan your budget on part-time income alone: the visa requires you to prove your living funds in advance.

When should I start?

Most programmes start in autumn (September/October). Plan backwards: shortlist programmes 9–12 months before, apply and secure admission in spring, then pay the first part of the tuition and start the police clearance about 3 months before your visa submission so that it is still valid. Because the decision is made in New Delhi and includes an interview and medical, leave several months for the visa stage.

After arrival: the residence permit

A D visa only lets you enter. After arriving, you must apply for a student residence permit at the competent Aliens and Immigration Department of the Decentralised Administration before your visa expires. Keep copies of every document you submitted for the visa, your university enrolment certificate and your insurance.

Common mistakes that delay or refuse Greek visas

  • Police clearance older than 3 months, or missing apostille or attestation
  • No direct email from the university to the consular section
  • Less than one-third of tuition paid
  • Funds below €400 per month, or deposits that cannot be explained
  • Insurance that does not cover the first year
  • A course with no clear link to your previous studies

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Greek embassy in Bangladesh? Visa applications are submitted at the Visa Application Center for Greece in Dhaka, and the Embassy of Greece in New Delhi decides them.

How much bank balance do I need? At least €400 for each month of study, plus proof that at least one-third of your tuition is paid.

Do I need a police clearance? Yes, the original, apostilled, attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka, and issued within 3 months of submission.

Is there an interview? Yes, an interview at the Greek Embassy is scheduled after you submit, followed by a medical examination.

Can my family join me? Family rules are separate from the student visa; ask us for your case before you plan.
